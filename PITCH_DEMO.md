# ASUH pitch recording path

Use one browser session so the check-in state naturally carries into the Family view. A browser refresh clears the in-session care actions; use it to reset between takes.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Older Adult — `/`**
   - Start with “Good morning, Maya.”
   - Click **Start check-in**.
   - Show one large-answer question, then **Answer by voice** or **Type instead**.
   - Complete the check-in (or cut between the first question and the completion view).

2. **Family Member — role switcher → Family Member**
   - Show Maya’s steady care-circle pulse and completed check-in.
   - Point to the care timeline and family coordination controls.

3. **Health — `/health`**
   - Show that ASUH gives general guidance, medication/refill access, and care-finder options.
   - Keep the non-diagnostic boundary visible.

4. **Companions — `/family/companions`**
   - Show Naya’s screening badge, rating, training, availability, support tags, and per-visit price.
   - Click **Request Naya’s visit**, then open the green booking confirmation.

5. **Visit follow-up — `/family/bookings/demo-visit`**
   - Show the structured, family-visible care update: arrival, activities, observed mood, and suggested follow-up.

6. **Community — `/community`**
   - Use this as the closing proof point for connection beyond the family home.

## Recording tips

- Record on desktop at 1440px wide for the cleanest layout; crop to your video frame afterwards.
- Use the language button before recording if the script will be in Bahasa Indonesia. It persists across navigation.
- The persistent role switcher is intentional for the pitch; it lets one recording move smoothly between the older adult and family perspectives.
- The prototype contains seeded demo data only. Do not describe companion screening, device data, healthcare availability, or SOS as live production services.
